A warehouse full of stories

Where sacks of coffee, cocoa and spices were once stacked to the roof, you now sleep between 130-year-old brick walls, oak beams and good design. We carefully converted the merchant house, kept the winches and hatches and rethought everything else: short distances, smart rooms, a team that knows Hamburg.

“This building once stored coffee and cocoa from all over the world. Today people come from all over the world – and we make sure they experience Hamburg like locals.”

Lena Asmussen, Founder and host

Good to know

All questions
Can I arrive at night?

Sure. Our front desk is staffed around the clock. Your room is ready from 2 pm, check-out is until noon – often later when you book direct.

Where can I park?

In the partner car park 300 metres away for €24 a day. Even better: train or bike – the Speicherstadt is low-traffic and everything is walkable.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, for €15 per night including bowl and blanket. Dogs are welcome in the canteen, but not on the rooftop.

Until when can I cancel for free?

With the flexible rate up to 48 hours before arrival. New Year’s Eve, packages and the saver rate have their own terms – they’re in your confirmation.

Is the hotel accessible?

A lift serves all floors, four Kontor Rooms have level-entry showers and wider doors. The rooftop has step-free access.

Getting here

  • Via the A1/A255 towards HafenCity. Parking in the Speicherstadt is scarce – we recommend the car park.
  • From Hamburg Hbf take the U1 to Meßberg (2 stops), then a 4-minute walk. Or the U3 to Baumwall.
  • From the airport take the S1 to Jungfernstieg, then the U4 to Überseequartier – about 35 minutes.
  • Partner car park 300 m away, €24 per day with hotel ticket.
